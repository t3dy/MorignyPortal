Abbey of Morigny

MEDIUM ABBEY · medieval; John's home house, early 14th c.

The Benedictine abbey near Étampes to which John was attached as monk and priest — the source of his identifying name. Beyond this basic fact of affiliation, the sources read for this portal do not treat the abbey's own institutional history in detail; that remains outside this portal's Phase 1 scope.
